The megacorp of the archdevil [Mammon](../dossiers/mammon), and the financial
engine of [Minauros](../locales/minauros), the commercial heart of
[Trell](../locales/trell). Highmark Ventures runs a financial system in which
credit crystals function as both currency and shackle: debt enslavement
masquerading as commerce.

Debts that cannot be recovered through official channels go to Highmark's
freelance debt collectors, who are feared throughout Minauros.

## Known associates

* [Vex Morradine](../dossiers/vex-morradine), now the [SPI](spi)'s Minauros
  contact, is a former Highmark debt collector&mdash;and goes very still
  whenever Highmark is mentioned.
* [Galloc](../dossiers/galloc) and [Preen](../dossiers/preen), the bodyguards
  of [Kerwin Ulvas](../dossiers/kerwin-ulvas), are believed to be Highmark
  freelancers. [Ulvan Transitional Assets](ulvan-transitional-assets)
  contracted its security through a shell company widely understood to be a
  Highmark proxy.
* The Abriymoch law firm [Vordache & Solm](vordache-solm) is linked to
  Mammon's people, and buys up debtors to bind them into servitude.
