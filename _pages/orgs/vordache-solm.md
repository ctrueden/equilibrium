A predatory corporate law firm of [Abriymoch](../locales/abriymoch), linked to
the people of [Mammon](../dossiers/mammon), and known for buying up debtors and
binding them into indefinite servitude. [Vex Morradine](../dossiers/vex-morradine)
recalls the firm selling a man into eleven years of labor over a debt that had
started as a few hundred coins.

Vordache & Solm represents [Serelda Voth](../dossiers/serelda-voth) in her tort
filing in the Abriymoch court against [Callie](../dossiers/callie), seeking two
hundred thousand gold pieces in damages and the submission of the cursed coffee
carafe as evidence. Its lawyers visited
[Ulvan Transitional Assets](ulvan-transitional-assets) multiple times, and had
been strategizing for weeks around the preemptive filing of
[Ercian & Brass](ercian-brass), the firm representing
[Kerwin Ulvas](../dossiers/kerwin-ulvas).
