---
title: The Noffindorfs
---

A family of gnome magical architects of considerable renown, and infamous for it. They build things with magic, and claim they never fail at their endeavors. Their servant is [Marigold Cogsworth](../dossiers/marigold-cogsworth).

They approached [Kerwin Ulvas](../dossiers/kerwin-ulvas) with a contract in [Curses and Carafes](../events/case-18).
