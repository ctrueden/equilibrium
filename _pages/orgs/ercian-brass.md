An [Abriymoch](../locales/abriymoch) law firm retained by
[Kerwin Ulvas](../dossiers/kerwin-ulvas), who boasted of their protection and
intended to countersue his ex-wife, [Serelda Voth](../dossiers/serelda-voth).

Ercian & Brass filed a preemptive brief arguing that voluntary and prolonged use
of the cursed coffee carafe diminished the original seller's liability. It is a
legal argument, not yet precedent&mdash;but one that could help
[Callie](../dossiers/callie)'s defense against Serelda's suit, brought by
[Vordache & Solm](vordache-solm).
