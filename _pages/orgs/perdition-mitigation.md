The megacorp of the archdevil [Baalzebul](../dossiers/baalzebul), ruler of
[Maladomini](../locales/maladomini). Perdition Mitigation is a vast insurance
conglomerate, profiting from the endless contracts, NDAs, and arbitration
disputes that plague [Trell](../locales/trell)'s corporate landscape.

When [Callie](../dossiers/callie) was sued by
[Serelda Voth](../dossiers/serelda-voth), [Mallory](../dossiers/mallory)
recommended Perdition Mitigation's office in
[Abriymoch](../locales/abriymoch) for her defense, suggesting that Callie's
family connections might already open that door.

See also: [Curses and Carafes](../events/case-18).
