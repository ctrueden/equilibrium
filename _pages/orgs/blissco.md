The megacorp of the archdevils [Belial](../dossiers/belial) and
[Fierna](../dossiers/fierna), rulers of [Phlegethos](../locales/phlegethos).
BlissCo peddles entertainment, luxury, and experiences, creating addictive
escapism for the masses while hoarding true pleasure for the elite. Its
influence extends into every vice and indulgence across [Trell](../locales/trell).

## Euphoria

BlissCo financed the resort town of [Euphoria](../locales/euphoria), and
contracted the [Noffindorfs](noffindorfs) to defeat the mind flayers who
infiltrated it. When it lost all communication with Euphoria, BlissCo hired the
[SPI](spi) to find out why: see
[Ingestion and Incoherency](../events/case-04). The imps
[Belifax](../dossiers/belifax), [Salazin](../dossiers/salazin) and
[Xoomish](../dossiers/xoomish) were BlissCo employees at the resort.

## BlissCo Legal

BlissCo's legal arm. The Voth family of [Abriymoch](../locales/abriymoch) is
linked to it, as are the Noffindorfs. In
[Curses and Carafes](../events/case-18), [Serelda Voth](../dossiers/serelda-voth)
sued [Callie](../dossiers/callie) over the cursed coffee carafe, and
[Kerwin Ulvas](../dossiers/kerwin-ulvas) of
[Ulvan Transitional Assets](ulvan-transitional-assets) was brokering the
sub-licensing of BlissCo-patented production spells to a Noffindorf
manufacturing subsidiary.
