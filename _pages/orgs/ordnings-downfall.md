A cult of giants and [goliaths](../creatures/goliaths) who resented their
place in the [Ordning](../creatures/giants#the-ordning), the caste structure
of giant society, gathered by the exiled cloud giant [Rhyzophus](../dossiers/rhyzophus).
They seized the [Storm Stables](../events/case-17), where the storm giants
breed their [behir](../creatures/behirs) mounts, and warded them against storm
giants. Rhyzophus promised his followers monstrous creatures, bred from
corrupted behir eggs in an ancient chamber beneath the Stables, with which to
overthrow the giant hierarchy, but was in it for his own power.

The agents broke the cult in [Case 17](../events/case-17), at
[Grohnea](../dossiers/grohnea)'s behest and with [Hyperion](../dossiers/hyperion)'s
blessing. Its surviving members swore allegiance to [Cal](../dossiers/cal),
and set out to redeem their honor by freeing the halflings of
[Xaryuvia](../locales/xaryuvia).

### Members

* Leader: [Rhyzophus](../dossiers/rhyzophus) (cloud giant), slain
* [Verthandi](../dossiers/verthandi) (stone giant dreamwalker), slain
* [Skalfang](../dossiers/skalfang) (frost giant), slain
* [Erka](../dossiers/erka) and [Ormar](../dossiers/ormar) (stone giant rockspeakers)
* [Mira Frostveins](../dossiers/mira-frostveins) (goliath)
* [Ulgrim Bearwalker](../dossiers/ulgrim-bearwalker) (goliath bear totem shaman)
* [Kureth Ironwill](../dossiers/kureth-ironwill) (goliath)
* [Moldakk](../dossiers/moldak) and [Grulthorn](../dossiers/grulthorn) (hill giants)
* Thok (hill giant), slain by Cal
* [Kavaki](../dossiers/kavaki) (goliath), who joined under duress and turned on Rhyzophus
