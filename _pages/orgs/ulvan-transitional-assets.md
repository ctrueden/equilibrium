The brokerage firm of [Kerwin Ulvas](../dossiers/kerwin-ulvas), dealing in
shipping, distressed acquisitions, and the legally murky licensing of
bloodlocked spells: the [Trell](../locales/trell) practice of binding patented
spells under infernal contract. At the time of Kerwin's death, he was brokering
the sub-licensing of a suite of [BlissCo](blissco)-patented production spells
to a [Noffindorf](noffindorfs) manufacturing subsidiary&mdash;a deal he
expected would make him wealthy even by his own standards.

The firm occupies the third floor of a recently renovated building in the
Harbor District of [Minauros](../locales/minauros), just down the street from
the [SPI](spi) branch office. Behind a large glass wall and a sign bearing a
steaming coffee mug, a busy staff of tieflings works in a deep, sulfurous,
sickeningly sweet odor: the emanations of Kerwin's cursed coffee carafe, whose
prolonged exposure left his employees unnaturally devoted to him.

After Kerwin was found dead, the Warlocks of the Archfiend sealed the building.
