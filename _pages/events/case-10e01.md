* [Vondal](../dossiers/vondal) leaves the Amulet of [Vecna](../dossiers/vecna)'s Lore in the safe in his house, along with his retirement savings. Then everyone met back up in the tunnel near the entrance to the old HQ.
* Lightning breath and shenanigans ensue. The old HQ has been consumed by the [Flux](../locales/flux). The gang escapes while [Ghensh](../dossiers/ghensh) serenades the guard (Lomagret Drakehead) and proceeds to get drunk with her.
* [Meroe](../dossiers/meroe) has a resurgence of flux taint. Vondal sees that [Panthea](../dossiers/panthea) also is still affected by the taint but has yet to tell the rest of the party.
* [Seirye](../dossiers/seirye-qin) wants to secure the [Cornucopia](../relics/cornucopia) by teleporting it away using a group ritual. The [SPI](../orgs/spi) agents do not necessarily agree, but Meroe is willing to do anything to get healed.
* The team heads to [Oasis](../locales/oasis) to prepare for the journey. Vondal plans to catch up in an hour.
