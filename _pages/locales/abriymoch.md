The great city of [Phlegethos](phlegethos), and the judicial center of
[Trell](trell), where the fates of corporations rise and fall through legal
warfare. Its iconic Crystalline Palace dominates the skyline&mdash;a gleaming
monument to wealth and power. This is where the boardrooms meet, where
contracts are signed, and where empires crumble under the weight of judicial
verdicts.

* [Serelda Voth](../dossiers/serelda-voth) comes from a minor branch of an
  Abriymoch family, and filed her tort action against
  [Callie](../dossiers/callie) in the Abriymoch court.
* During a business trip to Abriymoch,
  [Taelin Fosse](../dossiers/taelin-fosse)'s mind cleared long enough for her
  to write herself a letter and buy a protective mask from a local alchemist.
