An heirloom that has been in the Rockwell family for generations, and one of eight divine [relics](.) somehow central to the integrity of reality itself. It is said that locked within the Puzzlebox is the totality of Knowledge—but no one has ever managed to solve the puzzle to open it. The Puzzlebox was recently stolen from the Rockwells by [Alex Orbach](../dossiers/alex-orbach), a disgraced magical-scholar-turned-criminal, using forbidden [Flux](../locales/flux)-based magic.
