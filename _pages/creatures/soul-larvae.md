Glowing, butterfly-like spirits created by [Grohnea](../dossiers/grohnea) from
the souls of those she judges to richly deserve it, removing them from the
cycle of death and rebirth. They are not part of the gods' cycle of souls,
but made by someone with that specific power.

A soul egg set upon an unconscious or dead creature claims its soul, which
becomes a larva. When a larva is placed in someone's mouth, it crawls in and
back out, carrying away an affliction that the bearer can conceptualize as
the difference between who they are and who they were, leaving a butterfly
marked by what it took.
