Small, wily humanoids who dwell in tunnels beneath [the Mountain](../locales/mountain),
where they have long warred with the [dwarves](dwarves). Their clans are ruled
by chieftains, and some of their customs, such as the goblin blood oath,
are as binding as they are disgusting.
