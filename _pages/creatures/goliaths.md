Towering mountain-dwelling humanoids with stone-gray skin, kin to the
[giants](giants). In the giants' [Ordning](giants#the-ordning), goliaths rank
below even the hill giants, a grievance that drew many of them into
[Ordning's Downfall](../orgs/ordnings-downfall) at the Storm Stables. Some goliaths
carry traces of giant ancestry, such as frosty breath or an affinity for
storms, and goliath bear totem shamans commune with the spirits of ancient
giant-bears.
