A [stone giant](../creatures/giants) dreamwalker of
[Ordning's Downfall](../orgs/ordnings-downfall), whose very presence is alluring and
whose touch can enchant. [Ulgrim](ulgrim-bearwalker) had known her from a
past encounter, though not that she was at the Storm Stables. She spoke of
promises made to her by [Prometheus](prometheus).
