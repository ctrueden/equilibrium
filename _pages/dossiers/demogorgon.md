A huge two-headed monster formed when two of the three Furies merged with the
shadow [monkey](monkey) in the [Flux](../locales/flux)-corrupted roots beneath
SPI HQ ([16e06](../events/case-16e06)). It could draw in Flux energy to
duplicate itself. [Ozborn](oz) dealt the killing blow, only for
[Megaera](megaera) to raise it again as an undead horror
([16e08](../events/case-16e08)).
