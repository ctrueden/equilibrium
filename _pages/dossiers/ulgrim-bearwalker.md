An elderly [goliath](../creatures/giants) shaman who speaks with the spirits
of ancient giant-bears, and can manifest a great direbear spirit to protect
those around him. He believes the [Ordning](../creatures/giants) has disrupted
the natural order, which drew him to [Rhyzophus](rhyzophus)'s cult at the
[Storm Stables](../events/case-17), though he never cared for Rhyzophus
himself.

After the cult's fall, he swore allegiance to [Cal](cal) along with the
surviving cultists, and joined their quest of redemption to free the
halflings of [Xaryuvia](../locales/xaryuvia).
