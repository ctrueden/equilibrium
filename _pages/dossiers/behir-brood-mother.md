A larger and more ancient [behir](../creatures/behirs) than the others bred at
the [Storm Stables](../events/case-17), guarding a clutch of eggs.
[Rhyzophus](rhyzophus) stole her eggs to corrupt them in his ritual chamber,
and she has neither forgotten nor forgiven anyone involved.

She took a liking to [Vondal](vondal), her "morsel," who revealed the theft
to her, and after the cult's fall she regarded the Stables as hers.
