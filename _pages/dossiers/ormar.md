A [stone giant](../creatures/giants) rockspeaker of
[Ordning's Downfall](../orgs/ordnings-downfall), twin of [Erka](erka). The twins
crafted the physical components of [Rhyzophus](rhyzophus)'s enchantment
ritual, believing their arcane learning should earn them a higher place in
the Ordning.
