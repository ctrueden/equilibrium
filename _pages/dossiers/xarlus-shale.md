Bookkeeper for [Kerwin Ulvas](kerwin-ulvas) at Ulvan Transitional Assets,
hoping to one day become a partner in the firm. Lives in a sparse hostel
apartment, owning little more than a bed, a cooking pot, and three books.

When the agents broke the news of Kerwin's death ([18e06](../events/case-18e06)),
his first instinct was to suggest resurrecting his boss&mdash;unaware that Trell
requires a permit for that. He confirmed that the firm's security was
contracted through a shell company likely linked to Highmark Ventures, and that
[Serelda Voth](serelda-voth)'s lawyers had visited the office several times. The
only copy of the firm's financial ledger remains in the sealed office.

Like [Taelin](taelin-fosse), he is coming out from under the carafe's influence.
