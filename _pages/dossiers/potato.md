A cheerful, chatty tomato plant in a rooftop community garden in a rough
part of [Trell](../locales/trell), atop the hideout of [Galloc](galloc).
"My name's Potato. At least it's not Audrey Too."

When [Freki](freki) spoke with it ([18e05](../events/case-18e05)), Potato
proved a surprisingly reliable witness: its account of a singing tiefling
woman who could vanish at will pointed the agents to [Preen](preen). It
much prefers her company to Galloc's, who has never once sung to the plants.
Freki promised to bring it food, sang to it to encourage its growth, and had
his ravens drop roadkill compost on the garden, departing as its "Papa."
