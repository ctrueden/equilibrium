Former dock security in Trell's harbor district, and bodyguard to
[Kerwin Ulvas](kerwin-ulvas). Believed to freelance for Highmark Ventures,
the corporate arm of [Mammon](mammon).

He stepped out of an alley to engage the agents in friendly conversation
([18e05](../events/case-18e05)), while his partner [Preen](preen) lifted
[Taelin](taelin-fosse)'s pouch, carafe and all. [Ozborn](oz) later struck a
deal with him: the SPI keeps the case out of court and ensures the carafe
leaves town, in exchange for a tip on the artifact's location.
