One of the three Furies, who burst out of the [Flux](../locales/flux)-corrupted
roots beneath SPI HQ alongside the shadow [monkey](monkey)
([16e06](../events/case-16e06)). Her two sisters merged with the monkey into the
[Demogorgon](demogorgon), while Megaera fought on, until she became
[something else entirely](nerull) ([16e08](../events/case-16e08)).
